About

A little about me.

I play with systems to understand how they work.

I started in cybersecurity at 17 and have spent the past year on structured offensive training, labs and live bug hunting. I am in the second year of a BCA and working as a VAPT intern.

I am not much of a lab guy. I would rather play with real systems — you learn the most from poking at things that were never built for you to poke at. I have loved tech since I was a kid; computers always fascinated me, and now cybersecurity does.

My current focus is on web application security: finding real flaws, demonstrating their impact and writing reports developers can act on. I am working to level up my skills, with eJPT and OSCP as the next targets.
